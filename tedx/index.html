TEDxNIT Calicut Selection Task

Plan a TEDx Event

Work in small teams to create a realistic event plan for the NIT Calicut community. Build a clear written submission and be ready to present it.

Strict Budget

₹3,000 Limit

Plan realistic resource allocation and cost-effective ideas under strict financial constraints.

Assumed Venue

Classroom / Seminar

Standard campus classroom setup with basic AV and seating options.

Target Audience

NIT Calicut Community

Tailored specifically to engage students, peers, and campus members effectively.

Key Directives for Participants

01. Group Setup

Form groups of 3 to 5 members (aim for 4). Complete team alignment within 90 seconds.

02. Written Focus

Deliver actionable, tangible written plans on paper—not polished visual decks or digital slides.

03. Visible Adaptation

Keep the plan clear, practical, and ready to explain during your presentation.

04. Individual Ownership

Every team member must explicitly own at least one specific section, decision, or calculation.

Team Structure

Assign Roles Before You Start

Roles are required, not optional. Assign one role to every member and show each person’s contribution on the submitted sheet.

1

Concept & Audience Lead

Defines the event title, central purpose, target student audience, and reason for attending.

Key Deliverable Focus:

Event Title, Purpose Statement, Target Audience Reason

2

Execution & Logistics Lead

Plans the venue arrangement, operational tasks, resources, and setup logistics.

Key Deliverable Focus:

Execution Steps, Operations & Venue Setup

3

Audience Experience Lead

Designs interactive participation methods, Q&A formats, live engagement activities, and feedback mechanisms.

Key Deliverable Focus:

Interactive Participation Plan, Speaker Engagement

4

Risk & Practical Reasoning Lead

Manages the ₹3,000 budget, checks resource limits, and explains practical decisions.

Key Deliverable Focus:

₹3,000 Budget Breakdown and Practical Reasoning

5 Overlapping Support Role

Team Coordinator

Keeps the team aligned, connects the different sections, and ensures the final sheet is complete. Important: Anyone assuming coordination must still explicitly write and own a tangible portion of the written submission.

Requirement:

Must also own at least 1 concrete written section or decision

Hard Outputs Required

Submission Sheet Checklist

Prepare one clear, legible sheet with the required written outputs. Use this checklist to make sure it is complete and ready to present.

Required on the sheet

Write every member's full name and roll number, followed by their assigned role and contribution.

Inspiration Bank

Potential Event Themes

Choose or adapt one of these themes for your event concept. If your team cannot decide within 30 seconds, pick one quickly to proceed!

Perspective

Ideas That Change Us

Short talks paired with audience reflection on pivotal mindset shifts and transformative ideas.

Innovation

The Future We Build

Student lightning talks paired with an interactive idea wall focused on practical campus and societal changes.

Resilience

Failure, Reframed

Personal storytelling combined with guided peer discussions regarding setbacks, personal growth, and resilience.

Skills

Beyond the Classroom

Peer-led interactive sessions highlighting non-academic life skills, practical wisdom, and real-world learning.

Community

Small Changes, Big Impact

Mini-workshop format followed by a commitment activity focused on low-cost campus or community improvements.

Discussion

Uncomfortable Questions

Moderated discussion featuring anonymous audience submissions exploring taboo or rarely discussed campus topics.

Tech & Ethics

Technology and Humanity

Short perspectives followed by audience debate examining how modern technology influences daily human life.

Storytelling

Local Stories, Wider Lessons

Storytelling with facilitated Q&A deriving universal life and career lessons from local campus/regional experiences.

Reflection

Rethinking Success

Contrasting viewpoints and reflection activities exploring alternative definitions of achievement beyond grades and titles.

Challenge

What If We Tried?

Rapid-fire idea challenge and live audience voting structured around bold campus experiments.
